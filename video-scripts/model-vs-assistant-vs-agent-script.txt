TITLE: AI Model vs Assistant vs Agent — What's the Difference?

FORMAT: YouTube Short (~90 seconds)
TONE: Fast, punchy, conversational
EXAMPLE THREAD: Planning a Paris trip — same goal, escalating complexity

---

[HOOK — 0:00–0:05]
🎙️ TALKING HEAD
"Model, Assistant, Agent — everyone throws these words around.
Let me show you the difference with one simple example."

---

[PART 1: AI MODEL — 0:05–0:30]

🎙️ TALKING HEAD
"Think of an AI model as a superpowered brain locked inside a room.
It has read every book, every website, every research paper ever written.
All the knowledge in the world — but no hands, no eyes, no way to act."

🎬 SWITCH TO ANIMATION — show: a locked room, a glowing brain inside, a slot under the door
(voiceover continues)
"The only way to reach it? Slide a note under the door. It thinks. Slides one back."

🎬 ANIMATION — show: note sliding in → brain lights up → note sliding back out

🎙️ TALKING HEAD
"So you ask it — 'Which are the best hotels in Paris?'

It gives you a perfect answer. Names, ratings, neighbourhoods — all of it.
Because it was trained on that data."

🎬 ANIMATION — show: new note sliding in with "What's the cheapest flight to Paris next week?"
(voiceover continues)
"But now you ask — 'Can you find me the cheapest flight to Paris for next week?'"

🎙️ TALKING HEAD
"And it can't.

Not because it's dumb. But because it was trained on old data — it has no idea what flights cost today.
It can't open a browser. It can't check live prices.
It's locked in that room. The world moves. It doesn't."

---

[PART 2: AI ASSISTANT — 0:30–0:55]

🎙️ TALKING HEAD
"So we bring in an Assistant.
Think of it as a person standing right outside that door — with a phone, a browser, access to the internet."

🎬 SWITCH TO ANIMATION — show: same locked door, a person standing outside with a phone

🎙️ TALKING HEAD (voiceover)
"You say — 'Find me the cheapest flight to Paris next week.'
They go online, search, come back — 'Found it. Air France, $280, departs Friday.'

You say — 'Book it.'
They book it. Done."

🎬 ANIMATION — show: person walking back to the door, standing idle, waiting

(voiceover continues)
"Then they walk back and stand at that door again. Waiting.

Now you say — 'Search hotels near Eiffel Tower under $120.'
They do it. Come back. Stand there again. Waiting.

'Book the top one.' They book it. Back at the door. Waiting."

🎙️ TALKING HEAD
"You see the pattern?

Every. Single. Step. You have to tell them.
They will never do anything on their own.
One task. Done. Back at the door."

---

[PART 3: AI AGENT — 0:55–1:20]

🎙️ TALKING HEAD
"Now the Agent.

You don't give them steps.
You just say — 'Book me the best budget-friendly Paris trip for next week.'

They nod. And disappear."

🎬 SWITCH TO ANIMATION — show: agent walking confidently away from you, heading to the door on their own

(voiceover continues)
"On their own — they go to the door. Search the cheapest flights. Book the best one.
Go back to the door. Find hotels near the top spots. Book the best value one.
Build a full day-by-day itinerary. Check if visa is needed. Add everything to your calendar."

🎬 ANIMATION — show: rapid loop — agent going back and forth to the door across multiple tasks, all pieces falling into place: flight ticket ✓, hotel ✓, itinerary ✓

(voiceover continues)
"That loop — where it keeps going back to the brain on its own, task after task, until everything is done — that's the Agentic Loop.
That thinking it does before each step — that's Chain of Thought."

🎙️ TALKING HEAD
"And while it's doing all of that?

You're just sitting back. Relaxing.

You didn't search flights. You didn't compare hotels. You didn't plan a single thing.
You just gave one goal — and got back a full trip."

---

[OUTRO — 1:20–1:28]
🎬 ANIMATION — text cards popping in one by one
"Model = knows everything. Can't act. Can't browse live data.
Assistant = can act, but needs you to direct every step.
Agent = give it one goal. It figures out the rest."
