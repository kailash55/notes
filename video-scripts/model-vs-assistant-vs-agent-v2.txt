[PART 1: AI MODEL — 0:04–0:18]

🎙️ TALKING HEAD
"An AI Model is a superpowered brain locked in a room.
All the knowledge in the world — but no hands, no way to act."

🎬 ANIMATION — show: locked room, glowing brain, note sliding in and out
(voiceover)
"Ask it 'best hotels in Paris?' — perfect answer.
Ask it 'cheapest flight next week?' — it can't.
It's trained on old data. It can't browse. It can't check live prices.
Knowledge only. Zero action."

---

[PART 2: AI ASSISTANT — 0:18–0:38]

🎙️ TALKING HEAD
"An Assistant is a person standing outside that room — with a phone and internet."

🎬 ANIMATION — show: person outside the door with a phone
(voiceover)
"'Find cheapest flight to Paris.' — They search. Come back. 'Air France, $280.' You say book it. Done. Back at the door.
'Search hotels under $120.' Done. Back at the door.
'Book the top one.' — 'Sorry, no rooms available.'
Now it's your problem. You decide. You instruct. They go. Come back. Wait."

🎙️ TALKING HEAD
"Every step — you. Hit a wall — your problem.
They never decide. They just do."

---

[PART 3: AI AGENT — 0:38–0:55]

🎙️ TALKING HEAD
"An Agent? You just say — 'Book me a budget Paris trip for next week.' That's it."

🎬 ANIMATION — show: agent disappearing, rapidly looping to the door and back across tasks
(voiceover)
"They search flights. Book the best one. Find a hotel. Full? They find the next best — no questions asked.
Build the itinerary. Check visa. Add to calendar.
Loop after loop — that's the Agentic Loop.
Thinking before every step — that's Chain of Thought."

🎙️ TALKING HEAD
"You gave one goal. You get back a full trip.
While you were doing nothing."
